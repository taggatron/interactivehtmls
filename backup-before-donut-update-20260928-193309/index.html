    READING THE DATA

    Keep the context in view.

    This experience uses the values supplied with the original interactive. Component CO₂e allocations, material breakdowns, technical notes and images have not been independently verified. Some component estimates do not sum to the stated device total; totals are shown as supplied.

    The 2025–26 entries are treated as illustrative scenarios, excluded from comparisons by default. Storage configurations vary between generations. “Enclosure” groups steel and titanium/aluminium frames; “Shielding / thermal” groups aluminium and later copper systems. These are functional comparisons, not like-for-like raw material inventories.

    Material mass does not measure mined ore, extraction waste, recycled content or resource depletion. No extraction figures have been inferred.
