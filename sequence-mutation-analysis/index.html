Genomic Mutation Classification Guide

Clear reference for distinguishing mutation classes and functional consequences.

1. Mutation Classes (Structural Alteration)

SNV (Single Nucleotide Variant)

A single base pair substitution where one nucleotide is replaced by another (e.g. A → G, T → C). No change in sequence length.

Indel (Insertion / Deletion)

The addition or removal of one or more nucleotides. Changes the total sequence length and often disrupts downstream reading frames unless in multiples of 3.

Duplication

A segment of DNA is copied one or more times adjacent to the original sequence.

2. Functional Consequences (Protein Impact)

Silent (Synonymous)

The altered codon encodes the exact same amino acid due to redundancy/degeneracy of the genetic code. Protein function unchanged.

Missense

The substitution alters a codon to specify a different amino acid (e.g. Lys → Glu). Can alter folding, activity, or stability.

Nonsense

The mutation changes an amino-acid-specifying codon into a premature Stop codon (UAA, UAG, UGA), leading to a truncated, non-functional protein.

Frameshift

An indel not divisible by 3 shifts the triplet reading frame downstream, scrambling all subsequent amino acids until an inevitable premature stop.

In-Frame Indel

An insertion or deletion of exactly 3 nucleotides (or multiples of 3). Adds or removes intact amino acids without shifting the downstream frame.
