Standard Clinical Pedigree Nomenclature

International conventions for mapping genetic history

Symbol Standard Nomenclature Clinical Meaning
Square: Male Biological male. Displayed as an open square when unaffected.
Circle: Female Biological female. Displayed as an open circle when unaffected.
Diamond: Unspecified sex Used when sex is unknown, unrecorded, or in early prenatal counseling.
Unshaded: Unaffected individual Individual does not manifest the clinical phenotype under study.
Shaded (Magenta): Affected individual Individual displaying the clinical phenotype (e.g. Cystic Fibrosis or Huntington's).
Half-shaded / Centered Dot: Carrier Unaffected heterozygous carrier of an autosomal or X-linked recessive trait.
Slashed Symbol: Deceased individual Diagonal slash from bottom-left to top-right indicates deceased status.
Single Horizontal Line: Mating Connects reproductive partners.
Double Horizontal Line: Consanguinity Mating between blood relatives (e.g., first cousins).
Arrow: Proband (↗ P) The consultand or index case initiating the genetic evaluation.
